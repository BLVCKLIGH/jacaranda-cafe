Our story

Nomsa and David opened Jacaranda in 2019 in what used to be a florist, because two old jacaranda trees shade the pavement out front. Every October the terrace turns purple, and every October we're fully booked.

We buy single-origin beans from a small roaster in Maboneng, bake our bread and pastries before sunrise, and cook breakfast with eggs from a farm in Magaliesburg. The menu is short on purpose, so everything on it is made properly.

  • Locally roasted coffee
  • Free-range eggs
  • Baked on site daily
  • Oat, almond and soy milk at no extra cost

  • Sit in or on the terrace

    Forty seats inside and twenty-four outside under the trees. Dogs are welcome on the terrace.

  • Order ahead for pickup

    Add items from the menu, choose a time, and skip the queue at the counter.

  • Private bookings

    Hire the terrace for birthdays, baby showers or team breakfasts of up to 24 people.

  • Work from here

    Fast fibre Wi-Fi and plug points at every bench, all day.
